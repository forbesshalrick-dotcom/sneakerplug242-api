How to order

Three taps and you're eating.

1

Send the number

Message “2” and the bot knows you want the Family Bucket. Or tap it off the list it sends you.

2

Pick your side

It offers what the kitchen actually has going, then asks about drinks and extras.

3

Pickup or delivery

Collection gives you a time. Delivery asks the address and quotes the fee before you commit.

4

Pay and go

Bank transfer or cash on collection. The kitchen gets the ticket the second you confirm.

Order

Go on then.

This is the real thing — it prices the combo, adds the extras, works out delivery and gives you a total.

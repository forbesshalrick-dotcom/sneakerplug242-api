Why four

Because you should not have to translate.

A hardware man looking at a sneaker website has to do the work in his head. A car rental owner looking at a car rental website does not. So each demo is built in its own colours, its own type and its own words — that is also the honest answer to "will mine look like everybody else's?"

The bots are real

Not a video, not a click-through mockup. They hold state, keep a running total and print a genuine quote at the end.

The businesses are not

All four are invented, and every page says so at the top. Nobody's real shop is being used as a prop and no demo takes a real order.

Six different looks

Different fonts, colours, corner shapes and photography on each one. Your site is built to your trade, not stamped from a template.

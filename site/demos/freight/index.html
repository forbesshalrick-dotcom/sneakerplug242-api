Customs, handled

Send us the receipt. We clear it for you.

Customs needs to know what is in the box and what you paid for it. Upload the invoice here and we do the paperwork on your behalf — you never have to stand in that line.

    🪪
    First pickup only: bring a government ID

    Passport, driver’s licence or NIB card. It is a customs requirement the first time you collect — after that we know you and you just walk in.

    No invoice? Customs will put their own value on the goods, and that is almost always more than you actually paid. A screenshot of the order confirmation counts — it does not have to be pretty.

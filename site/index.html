Where the money leaks

You are not losing sales. You are losing replies.

Four places it goes. You will recognise at least one.

11:40 pm, Saturday

Somebody asks the price. Nobody answers.

They saw the post, they wanted it, they typed. By morning they have found it somewhere else. That message was money and it timed out.

Every day, forty times

The same three questions.

How much. You still have it. Where you at. You are a human search engine for your own stock, and it eats the hours you needed for actual work.

Ten times a week

“Send me pictures of what you have in a medium.”

Now somebody has to walk the shelf, remember what is left in that size, dig out the photos and send them one at a time. Nobody can hold a whole stockroom in their head, so the customer sees a fraction of what you actually have.

Whenever you put the phone down

Nothing can be bought without you.

No order goes through unless a person types it. Your business closes when you close your eyes, and it cannot grow past what two thumbs can handle.

Before you ask

Questions we get every week.

Will the bot sound like a robot to my customers?

It sounds like your business, because we write it in your words and your greetings. It knows your stock, your prices, your delivery areas and your rules. When it does not know something it says so and passes the chat to you instead of guessing. You can read a real conversation on the case study page and judge for yourself.

Half my customers write in Creole. Can it handle that?

Yes, and nobody has to ask it to. The bot reads what they wrote, works out the language and answers in that same one — English, Haitian Creole or Spanish — with the same prices, the same stock and the same payment details. It stays in that language for the rest of the conversation, right through to the message saying the driver is outside. There is no menu and nothing for the customer to pick.

What if it tells somebody the wrong price?

It does not carry its own price list. It reads the same live stock record you update, so a price is only wrong if it is wrong in your own system. You can also lock any product so the bot must hand it to a human before quoting.

I already have a website and I hate it. Can you fix that one?

Sometimes. Send us the link before you spend anything. If it can be saved we will tell you, and if starting over is cheaper than repairing it we will tell you that too, even though it is the smaller job.

Do I have to take the monthly plan?

On the Presence and Business tiers, no — you can take the keys and go. On Commerce and the AI sales layer, yes, minimum six months. Those are live systems talking to customers and money. We are not handing over something we cannot keep running and then watching it break with your name on it.

How much do I have to be involved?

One 30-minute call, one round of gathering your photos and prices, and two review passes. Most of it is us. The part only you can do is telling us what your customers actually ask.

Will this put me at the top of Google?

Nobody can promise that, and anybody who does is selling you something. What we do is build the pages so search engines can read them, set up your Google Business Profile properly, and put your real service and location words where they belong. That is process, not a ranking guarantee.

What happens if I stop paying the monthly?

The site and your domain stay yours — they are in your name, so nothing switches off out of spite. What stops is our work: updates, monitoring and bot tuning. On AI builds we give you 30 days' notice and a handover so nothing goes dark by surprise.

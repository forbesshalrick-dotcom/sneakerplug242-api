# Google Business Profile — Nightshift

Everything to paste in, in the order Google asks for it. Should take about ten
minutes once the domain resolves.

**Do this after the domain is live, not before.** Google checks that the website
works, and a profile pointing at a dead link gets flagged. Two placeholders still
need filling in below: the domain and the WhatsApp number.

---

## 1. Business name

```
Nightshift
```

Exactly that — nothing after it. Do **not** write "Nightshift Web Design Nassau".
Stuffing keywords into the name is the single most common reason a profile gets
suspended, and it is the one thing Google reliably catches.

---

## 2. Category

**Primary:**

```
Website designer
```

**Additional (add all three):**

```
Software company
Internet marketing service
Marketing agency
```

Primary category is what decides which searches you appear in, so it stays
`Website designer` — that is the phrase people here actually type.

---

## 3. Location

You have no shop and no walk-in trade, so:

- Choose **"I deliver goods and services to my customers"**
- Then **hide the address** — you do not want your home on a map
- **Service area:** `Nassau`, `New Providence`, `Paradise Island`

Adding the whole Bahamas as a service area is tempting and it hurts you. Google
ranks a tight service area higher for local searches, and Nassau is where the
work is.

---

## 4. Contact

```
Phone:   [YOUR REAL WHATSAPP NUMBER]
Website: https://[YOUR DOMAIN]
```

Use the WhatsApp number as the phone number. Somebody who finds you on a map at
nine at night will message before they ring, and it lands where you already are.

## 5. Hours

Set them **open 24 hours, seven days**.

This is not a gimmick for you — it is literally the argument you sell. A visitor
who sees a web designer open at 11pm has already understood the pitch before
they read a word.

## 9. Photos

Google shows profiles with photos far more often. You need at least a handful up
front:

- **Logo** — the clock mark on white, square
- **Cover** — the home page hero on a phone screen
- Screenshots of three or four demos on a phone
- One of you working. A face beats a stock photo every time here — this is
  Nassau, people buy from a person they can place.

Avoid stock imagery entirely. It reads as an overseas outfit, which is the one
thing you are not.

## 10. Questions & Answers

Google lets you post your own questions and answer them. Do it — you control the
first thing anybody reads, and it beats leaving it to whoever asks first.

**Q: How much does a website cost?**
```
Prices are published on our site rather than hidden behind a quote form. Sites
start at B$1,200 and the demos show what each exact build costs. You get a fixed
number in writing before anything begins.
```

**Q: What is a WhatsApp bot and do I need one?**

It answers your customers on WhatsApp when you cannot — sends photos and prices,

**Q: Do you work outside Nassau?**
```
We build for businesses across the Bahamas. Everything is done remotely, so the
Family Islands are no different — the bot does not care where you are.
```

## What to expect from verification

A service-area business with a hidden address usually gets **video verification**
rather than a postcard. Google asks you to record an unbroken clip showing:

- Your workspace and equipment
- Something proving the business is yours — a contract, an invoice, the site
  open on your screen
- You, on camera, saying who you are

One take, no cuts. It normally comes back within a few days. If it fails, it is
almost always because the video was edited or the business name did not match
what is written down elsewhere.

---

## After it is live

- **Ask three clients for a review** in the first month. Reviews move local
  ranking more than anything else you can control, and one review beats none by
  a mile.
- Post roughly monthly. It does not have to be clever.
- Reply to every review, including a bad one. A calm reply to a complaint sells
  better than a wall of five stars.

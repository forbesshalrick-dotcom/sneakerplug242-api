Why we lead with this

Most agencies show you work they did for somebody else. We are showing you our own money.

THE PLUG 242 is a real sneaker business in Nassau — real stock, real customers, real deliveries. We built the bot for it because we needed it, not because a client asked. Everything on the services page came out of this store first and was proven here before it was ever sold to anybody.

Problem

Every single message was answered by a person.

  • The same questions all day — how much, you have my size, you have this one in a 10
  • Somebody had to check the stock by hand before answering, every time
  • Messages came in overnight and on Sundays and sat there until somebody woke up
  • An order could not exist until a human typed it out
  • Busy days were the worst days, because that is exactly when replies got slowest

The business was not short on demand. It was short on the hours needed to answer it.

What we built

Jess — a bot that knows the stock.

Jess sits on WhatsApp and handles the conversation end to end. She reads the shoe code a customer types, checks what is actually on the shelf right now, confirms the size, quotes the real price, takes the order and passes it to delivery.

She knows the rules of the business, not just the products: which sizes convert between men's and women's, what shipping costs to which island, when a deposit is needed, and when to stop and pass the chat to a human.

The whole system, plainly

WhatsApp → Bot reads the message → Live stock list → Answer with real price and size → Order → Driver

No new app for the customer to download. No new number to learn. They message the same WhatsApp they always did — the difference is that now it answers.

What changed

The shop stopped closing when everybody went to sleep.

  • Repeat questions get answered instantly, at any hour, without anybody picking up a phone
  • Overnight messages turn into orders that are waiting in the morning instead of gone by morning
  • Stock answers come from the live count, so a customer is not told yes on a pair that sold an hour ago
  • Staff time moved off typing and onto picking, packing and delivering
  • The unusual conversations still reach a human — but now that is all a human handles
